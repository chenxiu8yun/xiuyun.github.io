About Mindfulness Yoga Studio

Jingxin Yoga Studio was established in 2018 and is located in a prime location in downtown Singapore. It boasts a spacious 100-ping (approximately 33 square meters) practice area equipped with professional yoga equipment and a comfortable rest area.

Our teaching philosophy is "harmony of body and mind, returning to one's true self." We are committed to providing professional and attentive yoga teaching services to every camper, helping them find inner peace, improve their physical condition, and enhance their quality of life through yoga practice.

The center regularly hosts unique events such as yoga salons and outdoor yoga, creating a yoga community for campers to exchange ideas, share experiences, and grow together.。
